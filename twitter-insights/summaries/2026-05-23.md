## Aaron Levie (@levie) `AI/ML`
*1 substantive tweet*

### Task Automation ≠ Job Elimination: Why AI Labor Predictions Keep Being Wrong `AI/ML`
- Levie argues the fundamental error in AI job-displacement predictions is conflating *task completion* with *job elimination* — even when AI can automate one or many tasks, the overall job definition expands to absorb new work
- Historical precedent: spreadsheets eliminated manual calculation tasks but didn't eliminate accountants — the job redefined around higher-order analysis; the same dynamic plays out with AI
- The implication is that AI productivity gains largely translate into higher output per worker rather than reduced headcount, at least in the near-to-medium term
- Matters for: founders building AI workforce tools (pitch around augmentation, not replacement), HR leaders planning org structures, investors modeling AI's actual labor market impact
