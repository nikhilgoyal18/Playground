### OpenAI's Inference Pricing Strategy as a Potential IPO Value Trap `Business`
- The four-step thesis: (1) commoditize users via cheap inference → (2) show revenue growth driven by that addiction → (3) IPO at $1T+ on growth narrative → (4) rugpull as token prices rise and enterprises enforce token budgets
- The insight is that current AI inference revenue may be structurally artificial: pricing below cost to drive adoption creates usage metrics that don't survive cost normalization
- Enterprise token budgets are the forcing function — once CFOs scrutinize AI spend, usage compresses dramatically unless value-per-token is demonstrably high; this is already visible in companies pulling back on LLM features after initial pilots
- Implication for investors: AI infrastructure companies showing fast revenue growth should be stress-tested against a scenario where inference prices normalize upward; the usage curves that justify $1T+ valuations may be price-elastic in ways that aren't yet visible at subsidized pricing

The 4-year degree ROI is increasingly questioned as AI shortens the skill-depreciation cycle.

Reddit's IPO moat of community trust and brand identity is its best defense but historically hard to defend against a cash-rich adjacent competitor.
