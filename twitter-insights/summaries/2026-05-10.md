### Munger on Envy as the Primary Human Driver `Business`
- Charlie Munger quote via David Senra: "It's not greed that drives the world, but envy." — 42 likes, 1 retweet.
- Munger's framing flips the standard economic assumption: greed is individualistic and finite (you want more for yourself), but envy is relational and comparative (you want what others have, specifically because they have it).
- Implication: products and marketing that leverage social comparison (not just aspiration) are tapping the stronger psychological force. This explains why status features outperform pure utility features in consumer behavior research.
- Decision relevance: product designers should audit their social proof and comparison mechanisms — "X people bought this" works through envy, not greed, and should be designed accordingly.
