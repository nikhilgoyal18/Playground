## Summary Themes

1. **Feature moats are dead in software — the four survivors are hardware, network effects, data, and compliance** — Lemkin's "rebuilt in an hour on Lovable" observation, combined with Aaron Levie's human-oversight point, define a new product strategy baseline: differentiate on the layer AI can't replicate, not on feature velocity.

2. **AI professional services adoption has crossed a social tipping point** — Harry Stebbings' lawyer anecdote ("can't admit we just got Legora") signals legal AI has moved from early adopter to expected baseline. Same dynamic is accelerating in accounting, consulting, and medical. The lag between "everyone knows about it" and "everyone is using it" just closed.

3. **SpaceX/Starlink is quietly building a sovereign infrastructure business** — Starlink schools in Bolivia + Chamath's "paid back the acquisition many times over" + the SpaceX government contract trajectory together suggest Starlink is becoming a global institutional internet provider, not a consumer broadband play. The Twitter acquisition framing was always wrong.

4. **IPO ARR quality is a 2026 risk to watch** — Sheel Mohnot's example of $26B ARR added with 90-day outs before an IPO is a preview of how AI-era growth metrics will be gamed. Public market investors who anchor on topline ARR without scrutinizing contract quality will get hurt.

5. **Replit's platform strategy is executing at scale** — #1 at SaaStr lead gen + Shopify partnership in the same week; Replit is moving from developer tool to commerce-enabling AI platform. The Shopify integration is the tell: they're targeting the business builder, not just the software engineer.
